# Cycle 335 — Jordan composite-selector boundary

## Target
Attack the strongest remaining PDT-II obligations by asking whether a strengthened, non-circular combination of homogeneous/self-dual local distinction cones and local tomography can determine a PDT composite law, select n=3, or force a same-input deviation from complex quantum theory.

## Exact hypotheses audited
Let each finite system have an ordered real vector space whose positive cone is homogeneous and self-dual (HSD), so that the single-system state geometry is Jordan-algebraic under the standard finite-dimensional hypotheses. Assume composites are non-signalling and locally tomographic. No value n=3 is inserted in the axioms. No specifically quantum tensor product is assumed.

## Result 335A — HSD + local tomography is not a PDT-native route (IMPORTED/KNOWN)
This route lies inside established Jordan/GPT reconstruction theory. Barnum and Wilce, using Hanche-Olsen's result and additional natural composite assumptions, obtain finite-dimensional complex quantum theory (with superselection structure) when the theory contains a qubit. Niestegge likewise studies local tomography as the final restriction from formally real Jordan structure toward complex quantum structure. Therefore HSD/Jordan structure plus local tomography cannot be relabelled as a novel PDT composition theorem.

## Result 335B — the known successful selector imports a dimension-specific seed (PROVED logical audit)
The Barnum-Wilce route explicitly requires that at least one system have qubit structure. Consequently it cannot serve as a non-circular PDT-native derivation of a privileged value n=3: its dimension-specific seed is n=2, and the resulting complex quantum theory contains systems of every finite Hilbert dimension, including 2, 3, 4, ... .

Thus even the strengthened known reconstruction does not establish `n=3 is the unique physically admissible dimension`.

## Exact n=1..12 stress
For ordinary complex quantum systems the real dimension of the Hermitian operator space is K(n)=n^2. Local tomography gives K(nm)=K(n)K(m)=(nm)^2. Hence every n in 1..12 satisfies the same multiplicative parameter identity:

There is no algebraic singularity at n=3 in this stress family.

## Result 335C — no same-input PDT/QM discriminator follows (PROVED implication failure)
A set of assumptions whose established realization includes ordinary complex quantum theory cannot, without an additional independent PDT postulate, entail P_PDT(O|I,R) != P_QM(O|I,R) for identical microscopic input I and resource window R. Choosing the complex-QM realization is a direct countermodel to that implication.

## Adversarial checks
1. **Composition circularity:** importing the complex quantum tensor product and then claiming its predictions as PDT-derived is rejected.
2. **Dimension circularity:** adding a rank-3, three-level, qutrit, or three-distinction seed would encode the desired answer and is rejected as an n=3 derivation.
3. **Local tomography alone:** already falsified in Cycle 329 as a unique selector; this cycle tests the stronger HSD/Jordan route rather than repeating that claim.
4. **Known reconstruction:** a successful reconstruction under extra hypotheses is evidence of prior art, not PDT novelty.
