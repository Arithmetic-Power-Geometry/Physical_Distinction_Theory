## 1. New candidate master principle: Reversible Distinction Sufficiency (RDS)

### RDS statement
For any admissible physical coarse-graining channel Phi and pair of states omega,sigma, equality of all resource-bounded distinction performance before and after Phi occurs iff the discarded degrees of freedom are recoverable for that distinction task.

Operationally, for the PDT distinction functional

Delta_R(omega,sigma)=sup_{a in A_R}|u_R(a,omega)-u_R(a,sigma)|,

we require

Delta_{R'}(Phi omega,Phi sigma) <= Delta_R(omega,sigma),

and equality for a pair iff there exists an admissible recovery Psi with Psi Phi omega=omega and Psi Phi sigma=sigma.

### Status
The forward data-processing inequality is already proved directly from pullback closure of admissible decision procedures. Equality under explicit recovery is proved. The converse (equality implies recovery) is not automatic in arbitrary GPTs and is therefore a new substantive principle if adopted.

## 2. Attempt to derive state resolution from RDS + one-bit rank

### Target
Show that every elementary state lies on a unique maximal binary distinction diameter:

omega = p alpha + (1-p) beta,

with alpha,beta perfectly distinguishable and midpoint independent of the pair.

### Attack
A bare rank-two capacity condition is insufficient: polygon models and non-Euclidean strictly convex balls remain counterexamples.

RDS strengthens the setting by demanding that equality of distinction under coarse-graining exactly characterize recoverability. Consider the coarse-graining E_ab=(I+S_ab)/2 for a reversible swap of a perfectly distinguishable pair alpha,beta. E_ab maps both alpha and beta to m_ab=(alpha+beta)/2.

If E_ab erases all distinguishability between alpha and beta, then Delta(E_ab alpha,E_ab beta)=0. However, this alone does not force m_ab to equal the global invariant state mu. Different pairs can have different midpoints in centrally nonuniform rank-two bodies.

### Result
RDS does NOT by itself derive common erasure midpoint or spectrality.

### Stronger principle isolated
We need a physically meaningful universal-erasure principle:

**Universal Distinction Erasure (UDE):** every complete unbiased erasure of a maximal elementary distinction yields the unique reversible-invariant state mu and leaves no recoverable side distinction inside the elementary system.

This is stronger than contractivity but weaker/more physical than directly postulating Hilbert geometry.

## 3. UDE + No-Residual-Distinction theorem

### Principle NRD
For an elementary rank-two system, once the maximal binary distinction and its bias are specified, no additional independent distinguishability remains in the elementary state.

Equivalent operational form: if two states give the same statistics for every effect generated by one maximal binary frame and its reversible orbit, then they are the same state.

### Theorem
Assume finite-dimensional compact convex elementary state space Omega, connected compact reversible group G acting transitively on pure states, maximal distinguishability rank two, UDE, and NRD. Then Omega is affinely equivalent to a Euclidean ball.

### Proof
1. Haar-average any pure state over G to obtain the unique invariant state mu.
2. Reversible equivalence of distinguishable pairs gives a swap S_ab exchanging alpha,beta.
3. E_ab=(I+S_ab)/2 sends alpha,beta to their midpoint m_ab.
4. UDE identifies m_ab=mu for every maximal pair, hence beta-mu=-(alpha-mu).
5. NRD gives, for every state omega, a maximal pair alpha,beta and p in [0,1] with omega=p alpha+(1-p) beta.
6. Therefore omega-mu=(2p-1)(alpha-mu): every state lies on a diameter through mu.
7. Points with |2p-1|<1 are interior points, so every boundary point is pure.
8. Pure-state transitivity therefore implies G acts transitively on the full boundary of K=Omega-mu.
9. Because G is compact, average any auxiliary inner product over G to obtain a G-invariant positive-definite inner product <.,.>_G.
10. Since boundary is one G-orbit, ||x||_G is constant on the boundary. Thus boundary(K) is a Euclidean sphere in this invariant inner product.
11. Hence K is a Euclidean ball in suitable coordinates; Omega is an ellipsoid affinely equivalent to B^n.

QED.

### Consequence
The parallelogram law and BQDC follow automatically from the induced Euclidean norm.

### Novelty boundary
The compact-group averaging and transitive-boundary rigidity are standard mathematics. The potentially novel PDT content is the operational UDE+NRD synthesis and its use to derive BQDC.

## 4. Can UDE + NRD themselves follow from a single deeper principle?

### Candidate: Complete Distinction Sufficiency (CDS)
A complete elementary state is exactly the minimal physical object required to reproduce every admissible distinction probability under all reversible contexts, with no inaccessible internal label that can later become operationally relevant.

This combines two requirements:
- **minimality:** no residual hidden distinction survives once the operational binary coordinate is fixed;
- **erasure uniqueness:** complete erasure removes that coordinate and leaves the unique invariant state.

### Derivation
Under rank two, CDS immediately implies NRD by minimality. For an unbiased erasure channel of any maximal pair, the output carries no remaining elementary distinction. By uniqueness of the completely distinction-free state under reversible transitivity, the output must be mu, giving UDE.

### Status
This is a real reduction in axiom count, but CDS is still a principle, not a theorem derived from scalar K_epsilon. It is more fundamental than separately postulating spectrality and common midpoint.

## 5. Composition: from elementary ball to full quantum theory

Once the elementary state space is B^n, we ask whether a PDT-native composition principle can force n=3 and complex QM.

### Candidate composition principle: Distinction Separability and Interaction Completeness (DSIC)
For independently prepared systems A,B:
1. product preparations exist;
2. local statistics jointly determine the composite state (local tomography);
3. reversible local operations embed faithfully;
4. there exists at least one continuous reversible interaction that produces non-product states from some product input;
5. every global distinction accessible without extra hidden sectors is generated by local and interactive distinction operations.

### Imported theorem
Masanes, Mueller, Perez-Garcia, Augusiak classify Euclidean-ball elementary systems with local tomography and continuous reversible dynamics. Except for the quantum two-qubit B^3 case, the nonquantum ball theories lack entanglement/interacting reversible dynamics.

Thus under DSIC, once B^n is derived:

B^n + local tomography + genuine continuous reversible interaction => n=3.

### Result
The elementary system is the Bloch ball B^3.

### Next closure
To get arbitrary finite-dimensional complex QM, one still needs a global state-space/composition theorem. A defensible route is:
- spectrality/homogeneous self-duality or Euclidean Jordan structure;
- existence of the qubit;
- suitable locally tomographic composites.
Established Barnum-Wilce/Hanche-Olsen-type results then select ordinary complex matrix algebras (up to known caveats/superselection assumptions).

### Can DSIC alone derive Jordan structure?
Not presently proved. Local tomography + interacting qubit does not by itself classify every higher-dimensional single-system state space.

### Minimal added global principle
**Subspace Distinction Closure (SDC):** every face generated by a maximal distinguishable set is itself a physically realizable subsystem of the same theory, with reversible symmetries acting transitively on equal-size frames, and composites preserve this face structure.

This is close in spirit to existing reconstruction axioms and must not be marketed as uniquely PDT unless derived from the distinction primitive.

### Status
Gap 2 is conditionally closed by CDS + DSIC + SDC plus imported reconstruction theorems. A completely PDT-only derivation of SDC/Jordan closure remains open.

## 6. Absolute calibration: attack on the horizon coefficient

### No-go already established
Scale-free distinction ordering cannot fix an absolute coefficient: K -> lambda K preserves the qualitative axioms but rescales kappa_0 and the horizon coefficient.

Therefore an absolute physical calibration is logically necessary.

### Candidate PDT-native calibration principle: One-Bit Thermodynamic Calibration (OBTC)
The irreversible erasure of one perfectly recoverable binary physical distinction at temperature T has minimal entropy export k_B ln 2 and minimal heat k_B T ln 2 in the reversible limit.

This is Landauer calibration, not new physics. It fixes the unit converting PDT bits to thermodynamic entropy:

dS = k_B ln 2 dK.

### Horizon derivation
If a local horizon saturates the Bekenstein entropy bound, then

K_H = A/(4 l_P^2 ln 2).

Equivalently

S_H = k_B A/(4 l_P^2).

### Status
The coefficient is exactly derived conditional on OBTC + Bekenstein saturation/black-hole thermodynamics. It is not derivable from scale-free distinction axioms alone.

### Stronger breakthrough target
Derive Bekenstein saturation from finite-resource screen recoverability rather than import it. No rigorous PDT-only proof found in this attack.

## 7. Dynamical distinction-stress law

### Candidate principle: Local Distinction Balance (LDB)
For every sufficiently small local causal horizon patch, the change in screen-accessible distinction entropy equals the distinction flux carried by physical stress-energy across that horizon in the reversible local-equilibrium limit:

k_B ln 2 dK_partial = delta Q/T.

This is a PDT translation of local Clausius balance.

### With horizon calibration
Using S = k_B ln 2 K and S = k_B A/(4 l_P^2), LDB becomes the local horizon entropy-balance premise used in Jacobson's derivation.

### Imported theorem
Requiring delta Q = T dS for all local Rindler horizons, with entropy proportional to area, yields the Einstein equation with a cosmological constant as an integration term.

Therefore

LDB + exact horizon calibration + local Lorentzian causal structure => Einstein equation.

### Status
Conditional closure only. LDB is still a physical postulate unless derived from a microscopic PDT dynamics.

## 8. Attempt to unify quantum and gravity principles further

The previous four principles can be grouped around one deeper statement:

### Distinction Conservation and Sufficiency Principle (DCSP)
A physically admissible theory must preserve exactly the recoverable content of distinctions under reversible evolution, must not create accessible distinction under coarse-graining without resource input, and must map complete erasure to the unique distinction-free state; when distinctions cross an inaccessible causal boundary, their lost accessibility is exactly accounted for by a thermodynamic boundary ledger.

DCSP has four projections:
1. reversible sufficiency -> equality/recovery structure;
2. complete erasure -> UDE and, with minimality, NRD;
3. composition -> local tomography/interacting distinction completeness;
4. boundary accounting -> entropy flux and horizon thermodynamics.

### What DCSP does NOT prove by itself
It does not mathematically force SDC/Jordan closure, the Bekenstein saturation coefficient, or the local Clausius relation without additional assumptions. Treating those as automatic would hide genuine physical content.
