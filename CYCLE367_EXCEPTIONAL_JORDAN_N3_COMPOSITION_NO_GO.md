# Cycle 367 — Exceptional-Jordan n=3 composition no-go

## Question
Can the exceptional Euclidean Jordan algebra J_3(O), whose matrices are intrinsically 3x3, provide a non-circular PDT-native derivation of n=3 together with a viable composition law?

## Candidate hypotheses
H1. Single-system physical distinctions are represented by a finite-dimensional homogeneous self-dual cone, hence (under the standard Koecher–Vinberg hypotheses) by a Euclidean Jordan algebra (EJA).
H2. The sought n=3 selector is the exceptional EJA J_3(O), the 27-real-dimensional algebra of 3x3 Hermitian octonionic matrices.
H3. Physical composites must admit a non-signalling EJA composite containing the factors as genuine nonclassical subsystems and supporting the usual product-state/product-effect interpretation.

## Result
**FALSIFIED as a route to PDT-II composition.** H2 does contain a distinguished rank/size 3, but that 3 is imported by choosing J_3(O); it is not derived from PDT distinction axioms. More decisively, known EJA-composition results rule out the required nontrivial composites: under the standard EJA composite conditions, no composite has the exceptional Jordan algebra as a direct summand, and a factor with an exceptional summand has no such composite unless the other factor is essentially classical (a direct sum of one-dimensional EJAs).

Therefore the exceptional 3x3 algebra cannot simultaneously do the two jobs PDT-II requires: (i) derive n=3 non-circularly and (ii) furnish a general PDT-native composition law for interacting/nonclassical systems.

## Dimension stress test n=1..12
The exceptional simple EJA exists only at the special 3x3 octonionic point; marking n=3 as exceptional does not constitute a derivation. For n != 3 the candidate J_n(O) is not a general EJA family because octonionic nonassociativity obstructs the matrix-Jordan construction beyond the exceptional case. Thus there is no dimension-parametric PDT law to test that predicts a peak at n=3; rather, n=3 is built into the imported algebraic classification.

## Counterexample / obstruction
The decisive obstruction is compositional rather than numerical: take A = J_3(O) and any genuinely nonclassical EJA B. Under the composite conditions studied by Barnum, Graydon and Wilce, the demanded EJA composite does not exist when A has an exceptional summand (apart from an essentially classical partner). Hence the proposed selector fails PDT target (1) before it can support targets (3)–(5).

## Same-input QM comparison
No P_PDT(O|I,R) != P_QM(O|I,R) follows. The exceptional algebra does not provide the required general composite experimental model, and importing it solely because it is 3x3 supplies no PDT-native probability rule. Any claimed numerical deviation would therefore be fabricated without an additional operational law.

## Prior-art boundary
- Koecher–Vinberg: homogeneous self-dual cones / symmetric cones correspond to formally real (Euclidean) Jordan algebra structure; this is established mathematics, not PDT novelty.
- Barnum & Wilce, *Local tomography and the Jordan structure of quantum theory* (Foundations of Physics 44, 2014; arXiv:1202.4513): Jordan structure plus local tomography and a qubit strongly selects ordinary complex quantum theory under their hypotheses.
- Barnum, Graydon & Wilce, *Composites and Categories of Euclidean Jordan Algebras*, Quantum 4, 359 (2020), DOI 10.22331/q-2020-11-08-359: exceptional EJA summands are obstructed in the relevant composites; simple non-exceptional EJA composites are sharply constrained.
