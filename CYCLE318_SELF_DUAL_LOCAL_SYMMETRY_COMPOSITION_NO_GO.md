# PDT-II Cycle 318 — Self-duality + local symmetry still do not select composition

## Target attacked
Highest-priority unresolved target: a PDT-native composite distinction law, strengthening Cycle 317 beyond local tomography and coherent crossnorm axioms.

## Candidate principle
Candidate selector: require a locally tomographic composite to (i) preserve the local state/effect identification (self-duality), (ii) respect local reversible/unitary symmetry, and (iii) agree with the local/product operational structure. Hope: these stronger geometric constraints might uniquely determine the correlated composite sector.

## Verdict
**FALSIFIED as a uniqueness principle / IMPORTED-KNOWN mechanism.**

Arai and Hayashi, *Non-standard entanglement structure of local unitary self-dual models as a saturated situation of repeatability in general probabilistic theories* (arXiv:2111.15019), explicitly show that self-duality does not uniquely determine the standard quantum entanglement/composite structure even after imposing local unitary symmetry; they construct infinitely many such composite structures. This directly defeats the proposed selector before any PDT-specific theorem can be claimed.

Reference: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.15019

## Why this matters for PDT-II
Cycles 315–317 showed that product multiplicativity, coherence, contraction, and local tomography leave correlated tensor directions underdetermined. The present check closes a natural stronger escape route: adding state/effect self-duality and the full local unitary symmetry still does not supply a unique correlated-composite law.

Therefore PDT cannot legitimately derive a unique P_PDT(O|I,R), an n=3 selector, or a distinctive composite inequality merely by appending self-duality/local symmetry to the existing composition axioms. Any surviving PDT-native composition principle must constrain something not already exhausted by these known operational/geometric requirements.

Repackaging these properties is not sufficient novelty. Priority should move to a genuinely PDT-native constraint on correlated distinctions, preferably one with an operationally measurable consequence under an explicitly fixed resource window.
