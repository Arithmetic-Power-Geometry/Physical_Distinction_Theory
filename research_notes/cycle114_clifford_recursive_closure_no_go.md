# Cycle 114 — Clifford recursive-closure no-go

## PDT-II target attacked
Targets (1) and (2): whether a PDT-native composition principle can force primitive same-sector closure `V x V -> V`, and thereby support a non-circular derivation of `n=3`.

## Candidate principle tested
Assume a Euclidean primitive sector `V` generates a finite-dimensional algebra `A`; composition is bilinear, associative and recursively closed in `A`; orthogonal relabellings of `V` extend to composition-preserving automorphisms; and the construction is dimension-uniform.

The claim that these requirements force `A = V`, or otherwise single out `n=3`, is **FALSIFIED**.

## Countermodel
For every finite `n`, the real Euclidean Clifford algebra `Cl_n` is generated by `V = R^n` and obeys `uv + vu = 2 <u,v>`. It is associative and has vector-space dimension `2^n`. Orthogonal transformations preserve the quadratic relation and extend to algebra automorphisms. Thus recursive finite-dimensional isotropic composition exists in every finite dimension, while vector-vector products generally leave the vector sector.

Smallest witnesses:
- `n=1`: `e1 e1 = 1`, a scalar, so `V` is not closed even though `Cl_1` is finite and associative.
- `n=2`: `e1 e2` is a bivector, not a vector, while `Cl_2` remains a finite recursively composable algebra.

Therefore recursive composability + associativity + finite dimensionality + orthogonal naturality **does not imply** `V x V -> V` and does not imply `n=3`.

## Validation
The cycle code represents Euclidean basis blades by bitmasks and multiplies them exactly, with `e_i^2=+1` and anticommutation for distinct generators.

- Exact generator-relation checks: `n=1..12`, zero failures.
- Exact generator-triple associativity checks: `n=1..12`, zero failures.
- Exhaustive all-blade associativity regression at `n=4`: zero failures.
- Exhaustive signed-permutation automorphism regression at `n=6`: zero failures.
- Sparse randomized stress test: 8,000 cases across `n=1..12,16,24,32,48,64,96,128`; zero associativity failures and zero signed-orthogonal equivariance failures.

The high-dimensional audit intentionally uses sparse basis blades rather than allocating the full `2^n` coefficient array.

## Prior-art boundary
Nothing about the Clifford construction is claimed as novel. Standard Clifford algebra facts include associativity, generation by a quadratic space, the relation `uv+vu=2<u,v>` up to sign convention, and dimension `2^n`. This cycle uses those facts as a countermodel and novelty guard only.
