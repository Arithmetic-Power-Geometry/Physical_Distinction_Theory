# PDT-II Cycle 327 — PDT-native generator via sequential/parallel closure: no-go

## Target
Attack the strongest open item after Cycle 326: can an independently named family of primitive distinction operations determine the physical PDT resource/composition law merely by closing those generators under sequential composition, parallel composition, identities, and relabelling?

## Candidate principle
Let G be a set of primitive distinction operations. Define Cl(G) as the smallest family containing G and identities and closed under sequential composition and tensor/parallel composition (and, if desired, reversible relabellings).

Candidate claim: these structural closure requirements uniquely determine the physical distinction resource family/composite law, and might thereby select n=3 or force a same-input PDT/QM prediction.

## Theorem — Generator-content underdetermination
The closure syntax does not select its generator content. Distinct generator sets can generate distinct closed operational families while satisfying exactly the same sequential/parallel/unit axioms.

### Minimal explicit witness
Use one classical bit X={0,1}. Let I be the identity stochastic map and F the deterministic bit flip.

G0={I}. Its sequential closure is {I}.

G1={I,F}. Since F∘F=I, its sequential closure is {I,F}.

For k independent bits, tensor closure gives respectively {I}^{tensor k} versus the family generated by independent local flips. Both constructions contain identities and are closed under sequential and parallel composition. Adding permutation/relabel symmetry does not identify the two resource families; the second contains nonidentity reversible dynamics and the first does not.

Therefore structural monoidal closure cannot determine which primitive operations are physical. The missing information is exactly the independently justified generator content.

Status: PROVED.

## Dimension stress
The witness embeds in every finite state space with n>=2 by acting on two distinguished states and fixing all remaining states. Thus the ambiguity holds for n=2,...,12 and every finite n>=2. n=1 is degenerate and has only the identity stochastic map. There is no exceptional behavior at n=3.

## Composite stress
The same obstruction survives whether parallel composition is represented by ordinary Cartesian products of classical systems, a chosen tensor product of vector spaces, or a GPT minimal/maximal/intermediate composite: once a tensor rule is supplied, different admissible generator sets still produce different closed subtheories. Conversely, the closure axioms do not themselves choose the tensor rule. Hence this candidate cannot repair the composite-law underdetermination found in earlier cycles.

## Pure/mixed and dynamics stress
The counterexample uses deterministic pure-state dynamics, so allowing mixtures cannot remove it. Convex closure simply gives conv{I} versus conv{I,F}, still distinct. Markovian closure does not remove it; history-aware/non-Markovian extensions only enlarge the space in which distinct generator choices can be made. Controlled-environment records and thermodynamic constraints likewise require extra admissibility data and are not supplied by the closure syntax.

## Prior-art guard
Sequential and parallel closure with an identity is standard monoidal/resource-theory structure, not PDT novelty. Resource theories are routinely formalized by monoidal structure, and quantum-channel resource theories explicitly assume closure of free maps under composition and tensor products. Therefore the structural mechanism is IMPORTED/KNOWN. The elementary underdetermination witness is recorded here only as a PDT-II route-closing argument, not as a novelty claim.

## Consequences
1. A PDT-native refinement generator cannot be obtained by saying only 'take primitives and close under composition/tensor'; the primitive set must itself be fixed by an independent PDT physical principle.
2. The axioms do not select n=3; the witness exists for every n>=2.
3. They do not define outcome probabilities and therefore cannot entail P_PDT(O|I,R) != P_QM(O|I,R).
4. They do not select minimal/maximal/intermediate GPT composites or injective/projective/intermediate tensor norms.
5. Any generator chosen because its closure yields a desired n, composite norm, or deviation is circular.
