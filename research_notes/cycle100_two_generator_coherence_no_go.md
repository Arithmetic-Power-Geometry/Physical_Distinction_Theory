# Cycle 100 — Two-generator coherence does not select `n = 3`

## Target attacked

PDT-II targets **(1) PDT-native composition law** and **(2) non-circular PDT-native `n=3` derivation**.

Candidate principle:

> If operational composition is parenthesization-independent for every history generated by at most two primitive distinction elements, then the composition law is forced to be associative strongly enough to select the three-dimensional distinction-vector sector.

## Result

**FALSIFIED.**

The octonions
\[
\mathbb O \cong \mathbb R\oplus\mathbb R^7
\]
are an exact counterexample. They are alternative but not associative. By Artin's theorem, every subalgebra generated by any two octonions is associative. Therefore *every two-generator rebracketing/path-coherence test can pass while global associativity fails*.

This is established algebra, not PDT novelty.

## Exact audit

Using the repository's Cayley–Dickson convention, all `21` unordered pairs of distinct imaginary basis units `e_i,e_j` were tested. For each pair the four vectors
\[
\{1,e_i,e_j,e_i e_j\}
\]
have rank four and form the corresponding quaternionic subalgebra.

For each of the `21` pair-generated bases, all `4^3 = 64` ordered basis triples were checked exactly with integer coordinates:

- pair-generated subalgebras tested: `21`
- exact basis associators tested: `1,344`
- nonzero associators found inside those two-generator subalgebras: `0`

Yet adding a third independent generator immediately exposes nonassociativity:
\[
[e_1,e_2,e_4]
=(e_1e_2)e_4-e_1(e_2e_4)
=2e_7.
\]

The witness norm is exactly `2`.

## Random adversarial regression

`1,000` random octonion generator pairs were closed numerically under multiplication. Every generated subalgebra had numerical dimension `4`. Within them, `12,000` random triples were tested:

- generated rank range: `4..4`
- residuals above `1e-9`: `0`
- maximum associator residual: `1.3173814904902171e-14`

This numerical evidence is only a regression check; the mathematical boundary follows from Artin's theorem.

## Dimension stress

The requested dimensions `1..12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128` are explicitly ledgered. This counterexample family only asserts the classical real normed-division-algebra imaginary-sector cases `n=1,3,7`. The single exact `n=7` counterexample is already sufficient to falsify an alleged universal `n=3` selector. No unsupported algebra is asserted in the other dimensions.

## Surviving theorem / sharpened PDT obligation

For an alternative algebra, any rebracketing test restricted to histories generated by at most two elements is blind to genuine nonassociativity. Thus, for the octonionic boundary,

\[
\boxed{\text{two-generator coherence}\not\Rightarrow\text{associativity}}
\]

and a genuinely three-generator witness is necessary.

This does **not** authorize PDT to postulate full associativity. The next prove-or-falsify obligation is narrower:

> Derive from PDT distinction/resource primitives an operational three-history or three-generator path-independence law, or show that such a law is false/resource-dependent.

If such a law is derived, it must then be checked against the `n=7` octonionic sector and against quantum/GPT/resource-theory composition. If the law is resource-indexed rather than exact, its quantitative defect may become a candidate route toward target (3), but no PDT-vs-QM probability difference is claimed here.

## Prior-art boundary

This cycle is **IMPORTED/KNOWN** at the algebraic level:

- John C. Baez, *The Octonions*, describes the hierarchy: power-associative (one generator), alternative (two generators), associative (three generators/all triples), and states Artin's theorem for alternative algebras.
  - https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/octonions/octonions.pdf
- Encyclopedia of Mathematics, *Alternative rings and algebras*, states that every two elements in an alternative algebra generate an associative subalgebra.
  - https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Alternative_rings_and_algebras
- Knarr & Stroppel (2023), *Subalgebras of Octonion Algebras*, gives modern structural context for octonion subalgebras.
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00335

No novelty is claimed for Artin's theorem, octonion alternativity, or quaternionic two-generator subalgebras.
