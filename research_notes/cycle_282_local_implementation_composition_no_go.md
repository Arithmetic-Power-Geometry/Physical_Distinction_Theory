# Cycle 282 — local implementation data do not determine composite controllability

## Target
PDT-II target (1): test whether enriching each local resource window from coarse `R` to a structured implementation object `(R, Lambda)` is sufficient to derive a unique composite admissible experiment family.

## Candidate statement attacked
**Candidate (false):** Given complete local implementation/control data `(R_A,Lambda_A)` and `(R_B,Lambda_B)`, the joint admissible/reachable family is uniquely fixed without additional interaction data.

## Exact counterexample
Take two qubits A and B. Give each subsystem the same full local Hamiltonian control algebra generated by `i X_A, i Y_A, i Z_A` and `i X_B, i Y_B, i Z_B`, with identical local resource budgets.

Construct two composites with identical local data:

1. **Noninteracting composite C0.** Joint generators contain only local terms `i sigma_j \otimes I` and `i I \otimes sigma_k`. Their Lie closure is `su(2) \oplus su(2)` (dimension 6). Reachable unitaries are local products and cannot create entanglement from product inputs.
2. **Interacting composite C1.** Add one coupling generator `i Z \otimes Z`. Commutators with the local controls generate the remaining two-body Pauli products; the dynamical Lie algebra is `su(4)` (dimension 15). Hence the reachable unitary family is strictly larger and contains entangling transformations.

Thus the local implementation objects are identical while the joint admissible/reachable families differ:

`(R_A,Lambda_A),(R_B,Lambda_B)` same, but `M_AB(C0) != M_AB(C1)`.

The obstruction embeds into every larger finite dimension by adjoining spectator levels/subspaces, so local implementation completeness does not by itself select a unique composite.

## Surviving theorem
**PROVED (elementary Lie-algebra counterexample):** Local implementation/control data do not determine composite controllability unless interaction/interface structure is additionally specified.

A viable PDT composition object therefore has to include a genuinely joint datum, schematically

`((R_A,Lambda_A),(R_B,Lambda_B), J_AB) -> (R_AB,Lambda_AB,M_AB)`

where `J_AB` specifies or is independently derived as the admissible interaction/interface structure. Merely enriching each local resource tuple separately cannot close the composition problem.

## Consequences
- Local structured implementation -> unique PDT composite: **FALSIFIED**.
- Local structured implementation -> n=3: **FALSIFIED** (counterexample is already qubit/qubit and embeds upward).
- Interaction/interface datum is necessary for general composition: **PROVED as a necessity/no-go statement** under the stated control model.
- A PDT-native law deriving `J_AB` from distinction principles: **OPEN**.
- Same-input PDT/QM quantitative deviation: **OPEN**.

## Prior-art boundary
Quantum controllability via the dynamical Lie algebra, and the fact that local controls plus an entangling interaction can enlarge the reachable algebra, are established quantum-control results. The PDT-specific value here is negative/architectural: it rules out another proposed shortcut to the missing PDT-native composition law. No novelty is claimed for the Lie-algebra facts.
