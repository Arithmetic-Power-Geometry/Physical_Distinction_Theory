# Cycle 228 — Universal-property composition does not solve the PDT seed-selector problem

Status: **PROVED (metatheorem / explicit countermodel)** / **FALSIFIED candidate selector** / **IMPORTED-KNOWN boundary** / **OPEN PDT-native replacement**

## Target attacked
PDT-II target (1), strongest survivor after Cycle 227: whether one can avoid independently deriving the physical seed/context class by declaring the PDT composite to be *the* tensor product characterized by a universal property.

## Exact hypotheses
Let a proposed composition prescription say that for objects A,B in an operational category C, the physical composite A ⊗ B is selected by a universal property relative to a declared class of admissible bilinear/bimorphic maps in C. The proposal is intended to determine physical composite admissibility without an independent PDT-native rule fixing the ambient morphisms/contexts.

## Theorem — universal-property selector underdetermination
A universal property can determine a composite uniquely up to isomorphism **inside a fixed category with a fixed morphism class**, but it cannot by itself select which operational category/morphism class is physically admissible. Consequently it cannot replace the Cycle-227 missing PDT-native seed selector.

### Explicit countermodel
Take the same finite carrier X_n={0,...,n-1}, n>=2, and its two-copy carrier X_n×X_n. Consider two operational completions sharing the same local reversible maps Sym(X_n):

1. C_L: joint reversible maps are generated only by products σ×τ of local permutations (and swaps/identity extensions where typed).
2. C_H: joint reversible maps are generated by C_L plus the controlled permutation C(x,y)=(x,y+x mod n).

Each completion can be closed under its own declared composition/extension rules and can be embedded in an ambient category in which the chosen product/tensor object satisfies the corresponding universal property. The universal property quantifies over morphisms *of that ambient category*. Since C is an admitted morphism in C_H but not C_L, the two physically interpreted admissibility structures remain different even though each can use a mathematically valid universal construction. Therefore saying “take the universal tensor/product” does not choose between C_L and C_H; the choice has moved into the morphism/context class.

Equivalently: uniqueness-up-to-isomorphism of a universal object is conditional on the category. It is not uniqueness of the physical category.

## Exact dimension stress
For n=1 the controlled map is trivial and there is no separation. For every n>=2, C is a bijection with inverse C^{-1}(x,y)=(x,y-x mod n), and C is not a product σ×τ because its second output depends on x. Thus the obstruction holds exactly for n=2,...,12 and algebraically for every finite n>=2. No floating-point computation is involved.

## Adversarial checks
- **Degenerate n=1:** correctly collapses.
- **Different norms:** irrelevant; the witness is algebraic/reachability-based.
- **Ancillas:** the separation embeds on the original two factors under identity extension.
- **Pure/mixed convexification:** convexifying the state sets does not make the missing reversible C locally generated.
- **Alternative reversible groups:** the theorem only needs two admissible ambient morphism classes with the same local data; permutations give the smallest exact witness.
- **Alternative tensor rules:** strengthens rather than defeats the point: a universal property is always relative to the declared morphisms/bimorphisms.
- **Same-input PDT/QM:** choosing a different ambient morphism category changes operational input/architecture, so a probability difference produced only this way does not satisfy the identical-input requirement.

## Prior-art boundary
This is category-theoretic/operational background, not PDT novelty. Tensor products of effect algebras are explicitly defined by a universal property relative to effect-algebra bihomomorphisms; operational probabilistic theories likewise begin by specifying systems/tests and their composition. GPT/OPT literature also contains inequivalent composite prescriptions (including failures of local discriminability and minimal-style operational completions). These facts support the boundary: universal constructions organize a specified theory; they do not physically derive the theory's admissible morphisms.

Representative audit targets: effect-algebra tensor products (including Lachman 2026); Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti operational probabilistic theories; D'Ariano–Erba–Perinotti on classical theories with nonstandard composition; Rolino–Erba–Tosini–Perinotti on minimal operational theories.

## n=3 consequence
The construction is uniform for every n>=2. A universal tensor/product prescription therefore cannot non-circularly select n=3 unless PDT first supplies additional physical content that selects the relevant category/morphisms and then proves that content forces n=3.

## Surviving theorem target
The composition problem cannot be solved by moving the missing physics into categorical vocabulary. PDT still needs a physical predicate S_PDT on primitive processes/contexts, stated prior to composite closure, such that

`physical data + R -> S_PDT -> primitive admissible morphisms -> universal/recursive closure`.

The universal/recursive stages may then be mathematically useful, but the novelty and falsifiability burden remains entirely in S_PDT.

## Breakthrough gate
**BREAKTHROUGH CANDIDATE: NO.** This is a decisive falsification of another apparently attractive composition shortcut.
