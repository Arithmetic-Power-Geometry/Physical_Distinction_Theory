# PDT dimension-selection prior-art audit

Status: **IMPORTED/KNOWN boundary identified; PDT-native n=3 theorem remains OPEN**

## Result of this audit

A tempting breakthrough route is to combine a Euclidean single-system state ball with composition, local tomography, reversible continuous dynamics, and the existence of entanglement/interactions and then claim that PDT newly selects dimension n=3.

That route is **not available as a PDT novelty claim**. Masanes, Müller, Pérez-García and Augusiak (J. Math. Phys. 55, 122203, 2014; DOI 10.1063/1.4903510; arXiv:1111.4060) already classify bipartite theories whose local state spaces are d-dimensional Euclidean balls under continuity/reversibility and local tomography, and show that the nonclassical entangled/interacting case selects the ordinary quantum two-qubit state space (d=3).

Müller and Masanes, *Quantum computation is the unique reversible circuit model for which bits are balls*, npj Quantum Information 4, 2018, further places generalized Euclidean-ball bits in the reconstruction literature and discusses the special role of d=3.

## Consequence for PDT

The existing PDT resolution-capacity construction may operationally estimate the dimension of an already-given finite-dimensional metric ball, but it cannot by itself select n=3. Adding generic entanglement + local tomography + continuous reversible interaction would substantially overlap established reconstruction results.

Therefore a defensible PDT-native dimension theorem must use an assumption expressed intrinsically in PDT's resource/distinction primitives that is not merely a renamed version of the established reconstruction assumptions, and it must derive a consequence stronger or differently scoped than the known Euclidean-ball classifications.

## Current proof-status ledger update

- Resolution-capacity slope identifies an existing metric dimension: **IMPORTED/KNOWN mathematical mechanism; PDT operational interpretation only.**
- CEU/CER/RDE alone select n=3: **FALSIFIED / insufficient.**
- Euclidean ball + local tomography + continuous reversible dynamics + entanglement selects the qubit case: **IMPORTED/KNOWN; do not claim PDT novelty.**
- PDT-native resource/distinction composition principle selecting n=3 without those imported reconstruction assumptions: **OPEN.**

## Next admissible research target

Search for a composition axiom stated directly in terms of resource-bounded distinguishability or distinction transport. It must have an independently motivated operational meaning, admit explicit counterexample testing in B^n for n != 3, and not encode entanglement/local tomography/interacting reversibility in disguised form.
