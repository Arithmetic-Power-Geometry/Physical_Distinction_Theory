# Cycle 068 — finite trace-word operator-completeness boundary

## Result

**Classification:** PROVED + IMPORTED/KNOWN + NUMERICALLY SUPPORTED.  
**Breakthrough candidate:** NO.

For a finite-dimensional Hermitian pair `(rho,sigma)`, define the mixed trace-word invariant

`M_w(rho,sigma) = Tr[w(rho,sigma)]`

for noncommutative words `w` over `{r,s}`.

For independent composites,

`rho_AB = rho_A ⊗ rho_B`, `sigma_AB = sigma_A ⊗ sigma_B`,

one has exactly

`M_w(rho_AB,sigma_AB) = M_w(rho_A,sigma_A) M_w(rho_B,sigma_B)`.

The proof is elementary: every word in the tensor-product pair factorizes into the tensor product of the corresponding subsystem words, and trace is multiplicative over tensor products.

## New search-boundary consequence

Cycle 067 established that the complete trace-word family is operator-complete enough to retain relational information lost by scalar/spectral/Petz summaries. Cycle 068 sharpens the boundary: at **fixed matrix dimension**, established invariant theory gives finite generating sets / finite degree bounds for trace invariants. Thus an infinite trace-word hierarchy is not needed merely to classify finite-dimensional matrix tuples up to the relevant conjugation orbit.

This is not PDT novelty. Procesi/Razmyslov trace-invariant theory shows that simultaneous-conjugation invariant rings are generated by traces of words, with finite degree bounds; Specht-type results connect trace-word equalities to unitary equivalence, and multivariable extensions cover families of operators.

Useful prior-art pointers:

- C. Procesi, invariant theory of matrices; traces of words generate simultaneous-conjugation invariants.
- Yu. P. Razmyslov, finite degree bounds for matrix invariants (bounds of order `n^2` are standard in the literature).
- Specht's theorem and multivariable extensions for unitary equivalence of matrix/operator families.
- Marcoux, Mastnak & Radjavi (2007), *An approximate, multivariable version of Specht's theorem*, Linear and Multilinear Algebra 55(2), 159–173, DOI: 10.1080/03081080600693285.

## Computational audit

The executable audit tests the exact tensor-factorization identity on dimensions `n=1..12` and `16,24,32,48,64,96,128`, random faithful mixed states, a second independent factor, and sampled words of lengths `1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,12`.

Result: **1332 tested identities, 0 failures**, maximum floating-point error `2.220446049250313e-16`.

These numerics are only regression evidence. The composition identity is algebraically proved, and the finite-completeness claim is imported theorem-level prior art.

## PDT implication

This closes another false breakthrough route:

> “Finite trace-word completeness by itself is a new PDT-native composition law.”

It is not. The surviving target is stricter: PDT must derive a **physically selected proper subfamily, quotient, filtration, or resource-indexed truncation** of operator invariants that (i) closes under composition or has a controlled composition law, (ii) survives counterexample search, and (iii) yields a new operational theorem or falsifiable prediction not already contained in standard matrix invariant theory.

No claim is made here for a same-input departure from quantum mechanics, a native `n=3` derivation, or a gravity/capacity law.
