# PDT theorem-status map

The repository follows the manuscript's status discipline.

- **PDT no-go:** scalar capacity does not determine geometry.
- **PDT no-go:** CEU+CER do not force BQDC / Euclidean geometry.
- **Conditional PDT theorem:** CEU+CER+RDE, with finite dimensionality, closed reversible group and maximal-pair coverage, yield an ellipsoid / Euclidean ball `B^n`.
- **PDT no-go / local dimension-selection:** for every `n>=2`, the Euclidean ball `B^n` satisfies the current local CEU/CER/RDE structure; therefore those local assumptions alone cannot select `n=3`.
- **Imported/known metric-entropy result + PDT operational corollary:** for a Euclidean `B^n`, resolution-scaled distinction capacity obeys `K_R(epsilon)=n log2(1/epsilon)+O(1)`, so the asymptotic capacity slope identifies an already-existing metric dimension `n`; this does **not** select `n=3`.
- **CONDITIONAL / IMPORTED-KNOWN dimension filter:** if an elementary Euclidean distinction space additionally carries a nontrivial bilinear antisymmetric normed vector product that is orthogonal to its inputs and satisfies the Jacobi identity, known cross-product classification leaves only `n=3`: nontrivial normed products occur in `n=3,7`, and the standard 7D octonionic product fails Jacobi. This identifies a precise sufficient interaction axiom but is **not** a PDT-native derivation until that axiom is physically justified without importing 3D quantum/rotation structure.
- **CONDITIONAL GDSD dimension filter / representation mathematics IMPORTED-KNOWN:** if an elementary Euclidean distinction space `V=R^n` with full connected reversible isotropy `SO(n)` obeys Generator–Distinction Self-Duality—an equivariant one-to-one identification `V -> so(V)` between primitive distinction directions and primitive infinitesimal reversible generators—then `n=dim so(n)=n(n-1)/2`, whose only positive-dimensional solution is `n=3`. The 3D converse is supplied by the standard Hodge/axial-vector identification. This uses a weaker algebraic assumption than the normed-cross-product route, but GDSD is still an unproved PDT physical axiom and the representation theorem is established mathematics; therefore it is **not** a breakthrough.
- **PDT no-go:** local data do not determine a unique composite.
- **Imported reconstruction:** `B^n -> B^3` only after external composite assumptions.
- **Conditional probability theorem:** `F(q)=q` under normalized positive orthogonal additivity and noncontextual dependence on `Q_D`.
- **Conditional Tsirelson theorem:** Euclidean local spaces + centered bilinear correlation + `||T||_op<=1` imply `2 sqrt(2)`.
- **Exact controlled-sector identities:** `c'=c chi`, pure/mixed record relations, phase-complete logarithmic generator.
- **PDT no-go / mixed-record scalar identifiability:** trace distinguishability `D_E` of the conditional environment states does not determine `|chi|` for mixed records. An explicit maximally mixed qubit-environment counterfamily keeps `D_E=0` while `|chi|` ranges continuously from 1 to 0.
- **PDT no-go / full conditional-output identifiability:** even complete knowledge of the pair `(rho_E^(0), rho_E^(1))` does not determine `chi` in controlled dephasing. For `eta=I_d/d`, all conditional outputs equal `I_d/d` for every controlled relative unitary, while `chi=Tr(V^dagger)/d` varies; explicit root-of-unity witnesses give identical output pairs with `|chi|=1` versus `0` for every `d>=2`. This is a rigorous PDT identifiability boundary, not presently claimed as novel quantum mathematics.
- **PROVED spectral coherence annulus:** under identical conditional outputs equal to `eta`, compatible coherence factors have radial floor `max(0,2 p_max-1)` and outer radius 1, where `p_max` is the largest eigenvalue. Historical novelty is not claimed pending stronger prior-art audit.
- **PROVED but non-sharp earlier corollary:** `|chi| >= max(0,2 Tr(eta^2)-1)` remains valid but has been superseded by the sharp purity theorem below.
- **PROVED + TIGHT sharp purity-only coherence floor:** writing `P=Tr(eta^2)`, identical conditional outputs imply `|chi| >= sqrt(max(0,2P-1))`. For every `P in [1/2,1]` equality is attained by a rank-two spectrum `(m,1-m,0,...)` with opposite phases, so no stronger universal lower bound can depend on purity alone. This is experimentally actionable and QM-compatible; historical novelty remains OPEN pending dedicated prior-art review.
- **PROVED operational quotient sufficiency criterion / factorization mathematics IMPORTED-KNOWN:** for a microscopic prediction map `Q(I)` and resource statistic `S_R(I)`, an exact resource-only predictor `Q=G_R o S_R` exists iff `Q` is constant on every fibre of `S_R`. In total-variation distance, any resource-only predictor has worst-case error at least one half of the largest within-fibre prediction diameter. This generalizes the controlled-record no-go into a universal PDT same-input kill test, but the underlying factorization/sufficiency mathematics is established and historical novelty is not claimed.
