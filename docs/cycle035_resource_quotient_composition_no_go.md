# Cycle 035 — Resource-quotient composition theorem and no-go

**Status:** PROVED linear-algebra theorem; IMPORTED/KNOWN mathematics; PDT composition consequence. **Not a breakthrough candidate.**

Let `N_R(V) <= V` and `N_R(W) <= W` be the locally operationally null subspaces under a declared resource window. If the composite accessible effects are exactly generated by products of locally accessible effects, then

`(V/N_R(V)) tensor (W/N_R(W)) ~= (V tensor W)/(N_R(V) tensor W + V tensor N_R(W))`.

The dimension is therefore

`q_AB = q_A q_B`,

where `q_A=dim(V)-dim N_R(V)` and similarly for B. The null-space dimension is

`n_AB = n_A dim(W) + dim(V) n_B - n_A n_B`.

This follows from the standard quotient-tensor isomorphism and the dimension formula for a sum of subspaces.

## Decisive composition boundary

The local quotients do **not** determine the physical composite unless PDT separately fixes the allowed composite effects. If product effects are guaranteed but independent global effects are also admitted, those global effects may separate directions lying in the product-null subspace. Thus the same local quotient data can coexist with different composite accessible dimensions between `q_A q_B` and `dim(V)dim(W)` in the linear operational model.

Smallest explicit witness: `V=W=R^2`, each local nullspace one-dimensional. Both local quotients are one-dimensional. Product-only accessibility gives composite quotient dimension 1. Adding one, two, or all three independent global functionals on the product-null directions gives dimensions 2, 3, or 4 without changing either local quotient.

Therefore no rule of the form

`Composite_R = F(local quotient A, local quotient B)`

is derivable from local resource quotients alone. A PDT-native composition law must specify an independent principle governing globally accessible effects/relations.

## Stress tests

`pdt_quotient_composition.py` audits dimensions 1–12, including zero-null, maximally nontrivial null, asymmetric and degenerate cases. `tests/test_cycle035_quotient_composition.py` verifies the exact dimension identity and the 2x2 smallest nonuniqueness witness. The theorem is analytic; the finite sweep is only an audit layer.

## Prior-art boundary

This is not claimed as new mathematics. Local tomography and the non-uniqueness of GPT composites are established topics. Hardy–Wootters show that real-vector-space quantum theory is bilocally rather than locally tomographic; GPT frameworks distinguish minimal/maximal tensor constructions; Barnum–Wilce use local tomography as substantive extra structure in reconstruction. The PDT contribution here is a precise kill test: **resource-quotient refinement does not itself solve composition.**

## Research consequence

Cycle 033's resource-quotient hierarchy remains valid for each specified system, but it cannot be promoted into a composite-system law without a new PDT primitive. The strongest surviving composition target is therefore to derive, rather than assume, which global distinction effects become admissible from the resource and interface structure.
