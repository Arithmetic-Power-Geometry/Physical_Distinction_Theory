# Cycle 034 — Resource monotonicity does not derive an area law

**Status:** PROVED no-go under stated hypotheses; IMPORTED/KNOWN elementary scaling mathematics; PDT consequence. **Not a breakthrough.**

## Claim killed
A generic PDT principle saying only that distinction/information capacity is finite and nondecreasing with available spatial scale/resource does **not** derive a gravitational area law.

Let a region be parametrized by a positive length scale `R`. Assume only:

1. `C(R)` is finite for every finite `R>0`;
2. `C(R)` is nondecreasing in `R`;
3. `C(R)>0` for `R>0`.

For every exponent `alpha>0` and constant `k>0`,

`C_alpha(R)=k R^alpha`

satisfies all three assumptions. Thus the same PDT resource assumptions admit line-like (`alpha=1`), area-like (`alpha=2`), volume-like (`alpha=3`), and arbitrary positive power-law capacity scaling. More generally, they admit many non-power monotone functions. Therefore monotonicity/finiteness alone cannot select `alpha=2` or the Bekenstein-Hawking coefficient.

## Stronger consequence
Even adding multiplicativity under independent composition does not by itself select an area exponent. If the composition variable is additive in log-scale, every exponential character produces a different exponent. A geometric or gravitational bridge is required to identify which physical quantity composes and how it maps to boundary area.

## Prior-art boundary
This is deliberately not presented as new gravitational mathematics. Existing entropy bounds and holographic/black-hole area laws use additional gravitational, thermodynamic, causal, or boundary assumptions. The PDT consequence is methodological: an area/capacity law cannot honestly be advertised as derived from resource finiteness/refinement alone.

## Kill test for future PDT gravity claims
Any proposed PDT derivation of `C proportional to A` must identify an assumption that excludes the explicit counterfamilies `C_alpha(R)=k R^alpha` for every `alpha != 2`, and must derive rather than insert the dimensional coefficient/Planck scale. If it cannot, the derivation is underdetermined.

## Research consequence
The gravity/capacity target remains OPEN. A defensible route must add independently motivated physics connecting operational distinction capacity to gravitational backreaction, causal screens/boundaries, energy-radius constraints, or another measurable structure. Importing a Bekenstein/holographic area bound as an axiom is allowed only with status IMPORTED/KNOWN, not PDT-native.
