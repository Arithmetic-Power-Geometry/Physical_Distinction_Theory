# PDT Cycle 310 — Simulation closure still does not let complete binary data reconstruct multi-outcome measurements

## Target
Strengthen Cycle 309 at its explicit open boundary. Impose the natural classical simulation closure generated by random mixing of measurements and stochastic post-processing/relabeling of outcomes. Ask whether a simulation-closed measurement theory is then uniquely determined by its complete binary effect data.

## Hypotheses
Let `Omega` be a finite-dimensional normalized convex state space with unit effect `u` and full effect set `E`. A meter/measurement is a finite tuple of effects summing to `u`. A measurement family `M` is **simulation closed** when it is closed under (i) classical randomization among allowed meters and (ii) stochastic post-processing of their outcomes. Assume every effect `e in E` occurs in an allowed dichotomic meter `{e,u-e}`; hence the theory has the complete binary response layer.

## Candidate principle
`simulation closure + complete binary effect accessibility` uniquely determines all finite-outcome measurements.

## Result
**FALSIFIED.** The simulation closure of all dichotomic meters is itself simulation closed, contains every dichotomic meter and therefore exposes the full effect set, but in general is a proper subset of the unrestricted measurement family. Thus two simulation-closed theories can have the same state space, the same complete set of effects, and the same binary response probabilities while disagreeing on higher-outcome measurements.

## Exact trit witness
Take `Omega = Delta_2` with coordinate effects `a(p)=p_1`, `b(p)=p_2`, `c(p)=p_3` and `a+b+c=u`.

Let

* `M_all` be the unrestricted classical finite measurement family;
* `M_2 = sim(D_2)`, the simulation closure of all dichotomic meters `D_2`.

Both are simulation closed. Because every effect `e` occurs in `{e,u-e}`, both induce exactly the same full effect set and agree on every binary response probability.

However the sharp trit meter `T={a,b,c}` is not in `M_2`.

### Direct proof
Any meter obtained by simulating dichotomic meters has effects

`A_j = sum_i lambda_i [nu_{j|0}^{(i)} e_i + nu_{j|1}^{(i)} (u-e_i)]`,

with `lambda_i>=0`, `sum_i lambda_i=1`, and each `nu^(i)` stochastic. Suppose this equals the sharp trit `T`.

Evaluate on the three pure states `delta_1,delta_2,delta_3`. `T` gives a deterministic, perfectly distinguishing three-symbol channel: on `delta_k`, outcome `k` occurs with probability one. A simulation protocol first chooses `i` independently of the unknown input, then applies a two-outcome meter, then classically post-processes its binary result. For every branch with `lambda_i>0`, convex averaging can yield a deterministic final distribution `delta_k` on input `delta_k` only if that branch itself yields `delta_k` with probability one. Hence each positively weighted dichotomic branch would have to perfectly distinguish all three pure inputs after a binary intermediate outcome. This is impossible: a stochastic map from two intermediate symbols cannot map three inputs to three distinct deterministic outputs unless the binary meter already assigns disjoint nonempty supports to all three inputs, but there are only two supports. Contradiction.

Therefore `T notin M_2` while `T in M_all`.

Equivalently, a sharp `n`-outcome classical measurement has zero-error distinguishability capacity `n`; classical randomization and post-processing cannot increase the per-branch zero-error alphabet beyond that of the simulated meter. Dichotomic simulation cannot create a sharp trit.

## Dimension stress
* `n=1`: degenerate.
* `n=2`: no higher sharp outcome is needed; the witness does not apply.
* `n=3`: smallest decisive witness above.
* `n=4,...,12`: embed the same trit face, or use the sharp `n`-outcome coordinate meter, which cannot be simulated from dichotomic meters by the same support argument.
* arbitrary finite `n>=3`: identical proof.

The obstruction is exact and combinatorial, so randomized high-dimensional testing is unnecessary for validity; numerical tests may be used only as regression checks, not as evidence replacing the proof.

## Prior-art check
**IMPORTED/KNOWN in substance.** Filippov et al., *Operational Restrictions in General Probabilistic Theories*, Foundations of Physics 50 (2020), explicitly require simulation closedness under mixing and post-processing and identify restrictions of type (R2) that do not restrict effects. Their prominent example is the class of **effectively dichotomic meters**, the simulation closure of dichotomic meters; every effect remains accessible although the meter set is truncated. The paper states that such restrictions are simulation closed and that effect restrictions alone are insufficient to capture all operational meter restrictions.

Therefore the mathematical mechanism is not claimed as a PDT invention. Its value here is to decisively close the stronger Cycle-309 reconstruction route under the exact closure condition that Cycle 309 left open.

## Consequences for PDT-II
1. Full pairwise distinction is insufficient to recover effects (Cycle 308).
2. Full binary response data are insufficient without closure (Cycle 309).
3. **Even full binary response data plus natural simulation closure are insufficient** (this cycle).
4. Therefore a PDT-native composition/measurement law cannot be reconstructed from the binary layer merely by imposing ordinary classical operational closure.
5. Any same-input PDT/QM inequality requiring genuinely multi-outcome compatibility still needs an independently derived PDT-native higher-order principle.
6. This route supplies no non-circular selector of `n=3`: the trit is a counterexample/witness dimension, not a derivation of physical dimension.
