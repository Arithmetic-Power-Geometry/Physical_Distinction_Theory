# Cycle 164 — Revelation-defect sign is not universal; exact sign boundary

Status: **PROVED / CONDITIONAL / FALSIFIED / IMPORTED-KNOWN / OPEN**

## Targets attacked
PDT-II (1) composition and (4) resource-refinement/revelation laws.

Cycle 163 left open the candidate correlation/redundancy defect

J_R(A:B) := C_A(R_A)+C_B(R_B)-C_AB(R_AB),

where C=log2(number of operational equivalence classes).

This cycle asks whether J_R(A:B) >= 0 can be a universal PDT law.

## Exact setup
Let X_AB be a finite admissible joint microscopic possibility set. Local resources induce partitions Pi_A and Pi_B of the corresponding local records. Pull these partitions back to X_AB. Let Pi_A ∧ Pi_B denote their common refinement: two joint microscopic states are equivalent iff they are equivalent under both local resources.

Let Pi_AB be the partition generated by the actual declared joint resource R_AB. Define

C_A=log2 |Pi_A|, C_B=log2 |Pi_B|, C_AB=log2 |Pi_AB|,
J=C_A+C_B-C_AB.

## Theorem 164A — nonnegative defect under no-emergent-joint-distinction hypothesis
Assume the joint resource reveals no distinctions beyond the paired local records, i.e. Pi_AB is no finer than the common refinement of the pulled-back local partitions. Then

|Pi_AB| <= |Pi_A ∧ Pi_B| <= |Pi_A| |Pi_B|,

hence

C_AB <= C_A+C_B

and therefore

J >= 0.

Proof: every block of the common refinement is indexed by a nonempty pair of local blocks, so the number of common-refinement blocks cannot exceed the Cartesian product of the two local block counts. A coarser/equal Pi_AB has no more blocks. Taking log2 gives the result. QED.

Equality requires, in addition, that every local block-pair occurs and the joint partition resolves exactly those pairs. Correlation/restricted support can make the inequality strict.

## Theorem 164B — universal nonnegativity is false
Remove the no-emergent-joint-distinction hypothesis.

Small decisive relational witness: take X_AB={00,01,10,11}. Let each declared local resource be completely blind, so

|Pi_A|=|Pi_B|=1, C_A=C_B=0.

Let the joint resource reveal parity only, with

Pi_AB={{00,11},{01,10}},

so |Pi_AB|=2 and C_AB=1. Therefore

J=0+0-1=-1.

Thus the universal claim J>=0 is **FALSIFIED**. Joint/relational resources can reveal a distinction invisible to both declared local resources.

This is not a beyond-QM effect and is not claimed as novel physics; it is elementary partition/information structure.

## Dimension stress family n=1..12 and beyond
For n binary components with every local resource blind but a global resource revealing total parity, each local capacity is 0 while the global capacity is 1 bit. Thus the naive sum-minus-global defect is -1 for every n>=2.

More strongly, a global resource may reveal k independent relational labels while all chosen local resources remain blind, giving negative defect -k relative to those local windows. Hence there is no dimension-independent universal nonnegative sign without an admissibility relation tying global tests to local tests.

At the opposite edge, for Cartesian product records and joint equivalence equal to the common refinement of fully revealing local partitions, C_AB=C_A+C_B and J=0. For correlated/restricted supports, J can be positive. Therefore all three signs/equality regimes are structurally possible once resource admissibility varies.

## Prior-art boundary
Partition refinement and partition lattices are established mathematics. Standard information theory likewise has subadditivity of joint Shannon entropy and mutual-information corrections under its probability hypotheses. Lattice entropy/submodularity is also established. None of these generic facts is PDT novelty.

The PDT-specific unresolved problem is not the algebraic inequality but whether PDT primitives independently force a physically meaningful admissibility relation between local and joint resource windows.

## Consequence for PDT-native composition
Cycle 164 gives a precise prove-or-falsify boundary:

- If PDT derives `Pi_AB <= common-refinement(Pi_A,Pi_B)` in the appropriate refinement convention from native resource/distinction principles, then a nonnegative revelation defect follows rigorously.
- If PDT permits emergent relational tests at R_AB that are unavailable under R_A and R_B, negative defect is possible and must not be mislabeled a contradiction.
- Therefore the sign of J cannot itself be adopted as an unconditional composition axiom.

A genuinely PDT-native composition law must specify how admissible joint resources are generated from local resources and which relational distinctions cost additional resource.
