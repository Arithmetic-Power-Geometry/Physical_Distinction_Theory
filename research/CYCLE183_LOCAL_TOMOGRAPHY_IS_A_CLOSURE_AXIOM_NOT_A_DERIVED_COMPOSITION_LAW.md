# Cycle 183 — Local tomography is a closure axiom, not a derived PDT composition law

Status: **PROVED / FALSIFIED / IMPORTED-KNOWN / OPEN**  
Breakthrough candidate: **NO**

## Target attacked
PDT-II target (1), strongest unresolved obligation after Cycle 182: whether a natural closure principle can be derived from local PDT distinction data and independent-product records rather than imported.

## Exact hypotheses
Let each finite system A have an operational real vector space V_A generated by its declared local states/effects/distinction statistics. Assume only:

1. local PDT data determine V_A and V_B;
2. independent preparations and product tests exist for A and B;
3. product statistics are bilinear;
4. normalization/positivity hold on allowed states/effects;
5. reversible local relabelings act covariantly.

No local-tomography axiom is assumed.

## Claim tested
Do 1–5 force the composite state to be uniquely determined by all product-test statistics, equivalently force the composite parameter space to contain no globally hidden sector?

**Answer: no.**

## Smallest abstract counterconstruction
Take any nontrivial local operational spaces V_A,V_B and form two candidate composites:

- C_min = V_A \otimes V_B;
- C_hidden = (V_A \otimes V_B) \oplus H, with H a nonzero real vector space.

Embed every declared product effect in the dual so that it annihilates H. Then all local and product-test statistics are identical in the two constructions, independent products still exist and are bilinear, and local reversible actions can be extended to H (trivially is enough) while preserving covariance. Nevertheless states differing only in their H coordinate cannot be distinguished by product tests. Therefore hypotheses 1–5 cannot prove H=0.

This is a logical underdetermination witness: it does not claim every arbitrary H-extension is a complete physical GPT. It proves that the stated PDT-local hypotheses alone contain no premise excluding a hidden global sector.

## Dimension stress test
The construction works for every pair of finite local dimensions d_A,d_B >= 1 by choosing dim H >= 1. Hence it covers n=1,...,12 and arbitrary higher finite n analytically; randomized numerical testing is unnecessary for the existence theorem. Degenerate one-state locals do not repair uniqueness: a hidden global coordinate can still be appended unless an additional closure/separation axiom forbids it.

## Consequence
Local tomography,

    global state equality iff all product-effect statistics agree,

is an additional composite closure/separation principle. It is not derivable from bare local distinctions + existence of independent products + bilinearity + covariance.

Thus adopting K_AB = K_A K_B, V_AB = V_A tensor V_B, or equivalent local-tomography language in PDT-II must be classified **IMPORTED/ASSUMED** unless PDT supplies a new physical argument excluding every hidden H sector.

## Prior-art boundary
This obstruction is known in operational-probabilistic foundations. Complex finite-dimensional quantum theory is locally tomographic, whereas real-vector-space quantum theory is a standard example where local tomography fails and bilocal information is needed (Hardy & Wootters, 2010). Barnum & Wilce show that local tomography is a substantive assumption in reconstruction results involving Jordan systems. Therefore the general fact that local tomography is an independent structural principle is **IMPORTED/KNOWN**, not a PDT breakthrough.

References checked this cycle:
- L. Hardy and W. K. Wootters, *Limited Holism and Real-Vector-Space Quantum Theory*, arXiv:1005.4870.
- H. Barnum and A. Wilce, *Local tomography and the Jordan structure of quantum theory*, arXiv:1202.4513.

## PDT-II implications
1. **Composition law:** OPEN. Local tomography cannot be promoted as PDT-native from current premises.
2. **n=3 derivation:** unchanged/open; choosing locally tomographic composition does not derive qutrit probabilities.
3. **Same-input PDT != QM prediction:** unchanged/open; a hidden sector is not a prediction until PDT derives its preparation, dynamics, and an experimentally accessible global effect.
4. **Resource/refinement laws:** candidate future route: ask whether a declared finite resource window operationally quotients H away; that would be a resource-relative equivalence theorem, not ontological elimination of H.
5. **Distinctive inequality:** no claim. A hidden sector alone is experimentally silent to product tests by construction; a PDT-native accessible global test would be required.
6. **Gravity/capacity:** no gravity law follows.

## Surviving theorem
**Composite-closure underdetermination theorem.** Under hypotheses 1–5 above, local tomography is not derivable: whenever one admissible bilinear product-statistics model exists, the local/product data alone cannot distinguish it from a formal extension by a nonzero sector annihilated by all declared product effects. Therefore any theorem excluding such sectors must invoke an additional separation, tomography, minimality, purification, capacity, or resource-completeness principle.
