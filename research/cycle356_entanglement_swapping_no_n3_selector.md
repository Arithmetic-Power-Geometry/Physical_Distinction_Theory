# Cycle 356 — Entanglement swapping is not an n=3 selector

## Status

**DECISIVE FALSIFICATION** of the candidate implication

> deterministic ideal entanglement swapping => local distinction dimension n=3.

This note does **not** claim a PDT breakthrough. It eliminates another genuinely joint/compositional route that cannot by itself solve PDT-II targets (1)–(3).

## Exact hypotheses

Let A,B,C,D be finite-dimensional complex systems with common local dimension d>=2. Assume:

1. AB and CD can each be prepared in a maximally entangled state |Phi_d> = d^{-1/2} sum_j |j j>.
2. B,C admit the generalized Bell measurement {|Phi_mn>} generated by Weyl operators X^m Z^n.
3. Conditioned on outcome (m,n), a reversible Weyl correction is available on A or D.
4. Composition is the ordinary complex-Hilbert tensor product.

Under these hypotheses, the standard Bell-basis identity rewrites

|Phi_d>_AB |Phi_d>_CD

as a uniform sum over Bell outcomes on BC paired with Weyl-related maximally entangled states on AD. Hence the BC outcome plus the corresponding reversible correction leaves AD in |Phi_d>. The construction exists for every finite d>=2.

## Counterexample

The smallest nontrivial witness is d=2: ordinary qubit entanglement swapping works, so the hypotheses do not imply d=3. The same construction at d=4 supplies the smallest higher-dimensional witness against uniqueness above 3. In fact the Weyl/Bell construction gives an infinite counterfamily for every finite d>=2.

Therefore:

- entanglement swapping => n=3: **FALSIFIED**;
- entanglement swapping => unique PDT composition: **FALSIFIED as an inference**;
- entanglement swapping => P_PDT(O|I,R) != P_QM(O|I,R): **FALSIFIED as an inference**, because ordinary QM already realizes the protocol under the stated microscopic inputs/resources.

## n=1..12 audit

n=1 is degenerate/trivial and is not a nontrivial entanglement-swapping system. For each n=2,...,12 the generalized Weyl operators and generalized Bell basis exist, giving the same exact construction. There is no n=3 singularity.

## Surviving PDT-II boundary

Entanglement swapping is stronger than a merely local or algebraic compositional axiom: it uses two composite resources, a joint distinction measurement, an outcome record, and conditional reversible dynamics. Its failure to select n=3 therefore sharpens the search boundary. A successful PDT-native selector must impose structure not already satisfied uniformly by finite-dimensional complex QM; in particular, it must constrain *which* joint distinctions/correlations are physically realizable as a function of the declared resource window, rather than merely requiring a standard joint protocol to exist.

## Prior-art guard

Entanglement swapping is established quantum-information structure (originating with Zukowski, Zeilinger, Horne and Ekert, 1993), and generalized high-dimensional Bell/Weyl constructions are standard qudit quantum information. No novelty is claimed for the protocol or its all-d existence. The only role here is adversarial elimination of this candidate route inside PDT-II.
