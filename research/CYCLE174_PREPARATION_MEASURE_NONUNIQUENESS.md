# Cycle 174 — Preparation-measure nonuniqueness

## Target attacked
PDT-II targets (2) and (3): whether a PDT-native preparation-dependent measure can be obtained from distinction structure without importing a quantum probability postulate.

## Candidate principle
A preparation is represented only by a resolved finite distinction quotient Q, its refinement relations, relabeling covariance, and independent product composition. Candidate claim: these data select a unique preparation-dependent probability measure mu on Q.

## Exact hypotheses
Let Q be a finite set of n operational distinction classes. A preparation measure is mu:Q->[0,1] with sum_q mu(q)=1. Require: (H1) finite additivity under coarse-graining; (H2) covariance under simultaneous relabeling of Q and the preparation labels; (H3) independent composites use product measures; (H4) continuity in preparation parameters; (H5) positivity/normalization.

## Theorem 174.1 — No unique measure from these hypotheses
For every n>=2, H1-H5 admit the full probability simplex Delta_{n-1}. Therefore the hypotheses do not select a unique PDT probability law.

### Proof
Choose any p=(p_1,...,p_n) in Delta_{n-1}. Define mu_p({i})=p_i and extend to every coarse-grained event E subset Q by mu_p(E)=sum_{i in E}p_i. H1, H5 hold immediately. Under a permutation sigma, transform the preparation by p'_sigma(i)=p(sigma^{-1}i); this is covariant, proving H2 without forcing uniformity. For independent systems p and q define (p tensor q)_(i,j)=p_i q_j, giving H3. Dependence on p is continuous, giving H4. Since arbitrary p is allowed, uniqueness fails. QED.

## Smallest decisive witness
n=2: p=(1/2,1/2) and q=(3/4,1/4) obey H1-H5 on the same resolved distinction quotient but disagree quantitatively.

## n=3 consequence
At n=3, p=(1/3,1/3,1/3) and q=(1/2,1/3,1/6) are both admissible. Hence a non-circular n=3 probability derivation cannot come from finite distinction/refinement/product structure alone.

## Dimension stress test
The proof is analytic for every finite n, hence covers n=1..12 exactly (n=1 is trivial/unique; n>=2 nonunique) and all higher finite dimensions. Random search cannot overturn the theorem; every sampled simplex point is another witness.

## Adversarial checks / edge cases
- Degenerate quotient n=1: unique delta measure; no predictive content.
- Pure preparations: simplex vertices remain allowed.
- Mixed preparations: convex combinations remain allowed.
- Reversible relabeling groups: covariance transports p; it does not require p to be invariant unless the preparation itself is invariant.
- Full transitive invariance of the *preparation* forces uniformity, but this is a special conditional case and cannot represent arbitrary preparations.
- Product composition preserves the freedom because arbitrary local p,q generate valid product measures.

## Same-input PDT vs QM consequence
A claimed P_PDT(O|I,R) != P_QM(O|I,R) cannot be generated by selecting an arbitrary alternative p from Delta_{n-1}. A PDT-specific selector must be independently derived from additional physical structure. Otherwise the discrepancy is stipulated, not predicted.

## Prior-art boundary
This is not claimed as a breakthrough. Standard probability theory already treats a probability measure as additional structure on an event algebra. In quantum logic, probability measures on projection lattices are likewise additional structure. Gleason-type results obtain Born-form uniqueness only after Hilbert/projector event structure plus orthogonal additivity/noncontextual measure assumptions (dimension >2). Importing those hypotheses would make an n=3 derivation quantum-logical rather than PDT-native.

## Status
- Theorem 174.1: PROVED.
- Claim that distinction/refinement/product structure uniquely selects preparation probabilities: FALSIFIED.
- Probability measures on event/quantum-logical structures and Gleason uniqueness under Hilbert assumptions: IMPORTED/KNOWN.
- Transitive-preparation symmetry -> uniform distribution: CONDITIONAL.
- PDT-native selector for arbitrary preparations: OPEN.
- Same-input PDT/QM quantitative disagreement: OPEN.
- BREAKTHROUGH CANDIDATE: NO.

## Strongest surviving obligation
Derive or falsify an additional PDT-native, preparation-sensitive scalar/vector/tensor object from operational distinction physics whose normalization gives a unique measure, while showing that the object is not merely a renamed density matrix, amplitude, Born weight, GPT state, or arbitrary prior. Any candidate must first survive n=2 and n=3 counterexample search before extension to n=12+.
