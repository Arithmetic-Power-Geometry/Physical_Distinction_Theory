# Cycle 199 — Linear resource revelation is submodular; superadditive synergy requires genuinely joint effects

## Target
PDT-II target (4), with consequences for targets (1) and (5): prove-or-falsify a resource-refinement/revelation law using only the currently defensible PDT quotient/effect-span structure.

## Status
**PROVED (conditional theorem)** / **FALSIFIED (unconditional positive-synergy claim inside the linear union-as-span model)** / **IMPORTED/KNOWN mathematical boundary** / **OPEN (PDT-native nonlinear/joint resource-generation law)**.

## Exact hypotheses
Let V be a finite-dimensional real vector space of microscopic states and let V* be its effect space. Fix a baseline resource window R with accessible effect span W <= V*. Let two additional resource packets A and B contribute effect subspaces U,V0 <= V*. Assume the combined accessible effect span is generated by ordinary linear union:

E(R+A) = W+U,
E(R+B) = W+V0,
E(R+A+B) = W+U+V0.

Define PDT distinction dimension

D(X) := dim E(X),

and marginal revelation

Delta(A|R) := D(R+A)-D(R).

No probability rule, Hilbert structure, norm, dynamics, or quantum postulate is assumed.

## Theorem 199.1 — Diminishing revelation
Under the hypotheses above,

Delta(A|R+B) <= Delta(A|R),

and symmetrically

Delta(B|R+A) <= Delta(B|R).

Equivalently,

D(R+A)+D(R+B) >= D(R)+D(R+A+B).

Thus distinction dimension is a monotone submodular rank function of linearly pooled resource packets.

### Proof
Pass to the quotient X := V*/W. Let Ubar=(U+W)/W and Vbar=(V0+W)/W. Then

Delta(A|R)=dim Ubar,
Delta(B|R)=dim Vbar,
D(R+A+B)-D(R)=dim(Ubar+Vbar).

The vector-space dimension identity gives

dim Ubar + dim Vbar = dim(Ubar+Vbar)+dim(Ubar intersection Vbar).

Therefore

Delta(A|R)+Delta(B|R) - [D(R+A+B)-D(R)]
= dim(Ubar intersection Vbar) >= 0.

Rearranging proves submodularity and diminishing revelation. QED.

## Theorem 199.2 — Exact overlap conservation identity
Define the baseline-relative overlap debt

Omega_R(A,B) := dim( ((U+W)/W) intersection ((V0+W)/W) ).

Then exactly

D(R+A+B)-D(R)
= Delta(A|R)+Delta(B|R)-Omega_R(A,B).

Hence 0 <= Omega_R(A,B) <= min{Delta(A|R),Delta(B|R)}.

This is an exact accounting identity, not an empirical law. It says that in the linear effect-span model, duplicate/redundant distinction directions are the only obstruction to additive revelation.

## Decisive falsification
Consider the candidate claim:

> Two ordinary PDT resource packets can exhibit strictly superadditive distinction revelation even when their joint window contains only the linear span of effects available from the two packets separately.

This is **FALSIFIED**.

Under the stated linear pooling hypothesis,

D(R+A+B)-D(R) <= Delta(A|R)+Delta(B|R),

with equality iff Ubar intersection Vbar = {0}.

Therefore any genuine positive synergy

D(R+A+B)-D(R) > Delta(A|R)+Delta(B|R)

requires failure of at least one hypothesis. In particular, the combined resource must generate genuinely joint effects not contained in W+U+V0, or the operational distinction functional must cease to be ordinary linear rank.

## Smallest decisive witnesses
### Redundancy
Take V*=R, W=0, U=V0=R. Then

Delta(A|R)=1,
Delta(B|R)=1,
D(R+A+B)-D(R)=1,
Omega=1.

Naive additive conservation 1+1=2 is false; exact overlap-corrected accounting gives 1+1-1=1.

### Zero-overlap equality
Take V*=R^2, W=0, U=span(e1), V0=span(e2). Then both marginal revelations equal 1, joint revelation is 2, and Omega=0.

### Baseline-hidden redundancy
Take V*=R^3, W=span(e1), U=span(e1,e2), V0=span(e2,e3). Relative to W, the two packets overlap in the e2 direction. Marginals are 1 and 2, joint revelation is 2, and Omega=1.

These witnesses show that overlap must be computed after quotienting the already-accessible baseline.

## Dimension stress test
The theorem is exact and dimension-independent. It therefore holds without numerical approximation for ambient dimensions n=1,...,12 and every finite n. Edge cases:

- W=V*: every marginal is zero;
- U=0 or V0=0: the corresponding marginal is zero;
- U <= W: A reveals nothing;
- U=V0: maximal redundancy after quotienting W;
- direct-sum quotient images: exact additivity;
- arbitrary intersections: exact overlap correction above.

Changing norms does not affect the rank identity. Any invertible reversible transformation g:V*->V* preserves all dimensions and intersections, so the law is covariant under arbitrary invertible reversible groups. Tensor-product systems obey the theorem whenever the declared composite resource window is still obtained by linear pooling of its accessible effect subspaces.

Pure/mixed state labels do not affect this structural result because D is defined by the accessible effect span. Markovian/non-Markovian dynamics and controlled-environment records also do not invalidate the theorem if their induced accessible effects are first collected into fixed subspaces and joint resources add no new effects beyond linear pooling. If joint control creates a new effect inaccessible to either packet separately, that is precisely outside the theorem's hypotheses and is the surviving synergy route.

## Adversarial checks
1. **Duplicate effects:** absorbed by Omega; no false additivity.
2. **Effects already present in baseline:** removed correctly by quotienting W.
3. **Different bases/norms:** dimensions and intersection dimensions are invariant.
4. **Reversible maps:** simultaneous invertible maps preserve the identity.
5. **Degenerate windows:** all zero/full cases satisfy the formula.
6. **Composite systems:** hidden/global sectors from Cycles 196-198 do not create synergy unless a joint resource actually grants effects on those sectors; merely having hidden state coordinates is insufficient.
7. **Noncommuting sequential protocols:** not covered by a static union-as-span model; Cycle 188 already established order dependence there. No static submodularity claim is exported to that setting without an explicit reduction to fixed effect subspaces.

## Prior-art boundary
This theorem must **not** be promoted as PDT novelty. The mathematical content is the standard dimension formula for sums/intersections of vector subspaces and the submodularity of representable matroid/polymatroid rank functions. Linear representable polymatroids are explicitly defined by ranks of sums of subspaces, and rank submodularity is standard. Restricted-measurement distinguishability and resource theories of distinguishability are also established quantum-information topics.

Accordingly, the value of this cycle is a rigorous PDT boundary and bookkeeping law, not a breakthrough claim.

Relevant prior-art classes:
- vector-space dimension modular identity;
- representable matroids/polymatroids and submodular rank;
- restricted-measurement distinguishability;
- quantum resource theories of distinguishability.

## Consequence for PDT-II
The strongest surviving resource-generation question is now sharply falsifiable:

> Can PDT derive, from physical rather than declarative assumptions, a joint-resource effect J(A,B) not contained in E(R+A)+E(R+B), and thereby predict a controlled violation of the static linear submodularity bound under a precisely matched microscopic input/resource protocol?

A violation would not by itself be a breakthrough: contextual measurement, interaction-enabled observables, adaptive sensing, and GPT composition provide known mechanisms for joint accessibility. A PDT candidate must derive the joint effect and its quantitative consequence independently, then survive same-input QM/GPT comparison.
