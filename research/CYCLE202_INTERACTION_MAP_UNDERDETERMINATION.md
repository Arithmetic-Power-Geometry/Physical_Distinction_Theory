# Cycle 202 — Interaction-map underdetermination no-go

## Status

**PROVED (conditional no-go); FALSIFIED (generic interaction map as a derivation); IMPORTED/KNOWN boundary; OPEN (PDT-native microscopic interaction law).**

BREAKTHROUGH CANDIDATE: **NO**.

## Target

After Cycles 199–201, test whether replacing an abstract closure operator by a binary microscopic-looking interaction map `G(A,B)` is sufficient to derive PDT composition or joint distinction generation.

## Exact hypotheses tested

Let X be a finite distinction set. An interaction generator G assigns to each unordered pair of resource sets A,B a generated set G(A,B) subseteq X. Impose only the natural structural conditions:

1. symmetry: G(A,B)=G(B,A);
2. null interaction: G(A,empty)=empty;
3. locality of support: G(A,B) depends only on A and B;
4. monotonicity: A subseteq A', B subseteq B' implies G(A,B) subseteq G(A',B');
5. generated distinctions are then added to A union B.

These conditions are deliberately weaker than specifying a Hamiltonian, reaction relation, channel, or concrete physical coupling.

## Smallest decisive counterexample

Take X={a,b,c}. Define two interaction maps.

**G0:** G0(A,B)=empty for all A,B.

**G1:** G1(A,B)={c} iff `(a in A and b in B) or (b in A and a in B)`; otherwise empty.

Both satisfy symmetry, null interaction, support locality, and monotonicity. They agree on all one-resource data and on the underlying distinction set. But

- G0({a},{b})=empty;
- G1({a},{b})={c}.

Therefore the structural axioms do not determine whether the composite generates a new distinction. Calling G 'microscopic' does not remove the underdetermination unless its values are derived from independent microscopic dynamics.

## Theorem 202.1 — Generic interaction-map non-uniqueness

For every finite distinction universe containing at least three elements, the five hypotheses above admit at least two interaction generators that agree on isolated-resource behavior but disagree on joint generation.

### Proof

Embed the witness {a,b,c} in X. Let G0 be identically empty. Let G1 activate c exactly when opposite arguments contain a and b. Symmetry and null interaction are immediate. If either argument is enlarged, an already satisfied activation condition remains satisfied, proving monotonicity. Both maps generate nothing when either resource is absent and hence have identical isolated-resource behavior. Their values on ({a},{b}) differ. QED.

The construction embeds unchanged in dimensions n=3,...,12 and all higher finite n by adjoining inert distinctions.

## Consequences for PDT-II

1. A symbol `G_R(A,B)` is not yet a PDT-native composition law.
2. Structural properties such as symmetry, monotonicity, covariance, or closure can constrain an interaction law but cannot select it from local data alone.
3. To claim derivation, PDT must specify independently motivated microscopic dynamics or an operational mechanism from which G_R is calculable.
4. Any generated-effect synergy or experimentally distinctive inequality depending on an unspecified G_R remains CONDITIONAL.
5. This cycle does not support a PDT–QM probability difference.

## Prior-art boundary

Interaction/reaction maps and compositional algebras are established mathematical structures. Milner's action structures already combine composition/product with a reaction relation and emphasize effects of interactions; later action calculi and process algebras likewise parameterize interaction by generators/controls. Quantum information also uses interaction algebras generated by concrete system-environment coupling operators. Therefore introducing an interaction map/algebra is not itself PDT novelty. The potential PDT contribution must lie in a new physically derived law selecting the map and yielding a new theorem or falsifiable prediction.

## Strongest surviving obligation

Do not add further generic algebraic axioms merely to force uniqueness. Attack a concrete candidate microscopic rule next. A defensible candidate must specify its primitive input independently of the desired n=3/Born/composition result, compute G_R from that input, and survive same-input comparison with QM/GPT/resource theories.
