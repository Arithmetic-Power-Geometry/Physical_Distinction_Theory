# Cycle 196 — Local quotient data do not determine the composite

## Target
PDT-II target (1): PDT-native composition law.

## Status
**PROVED (no-go, conditional on the current quotient formalism) / FALSIFIED (uniqueness from local quotient data alone) / IMPORTED-KNOWN boundary / OPEN (additional PDT-native composition axiom).**

**BREAKTHROUGH CANDIDATE: NO.**

## Claim tested
Can the current PDT local resource-relative quotient data `(Q_A,Q_B,E_A,E_B)` determine a unique composite distinction space without adding a genuinely new composition principle?

## Theorem (non-uniqueness of composite from local quotient data)
Let finite-dimensional local observable quotient spaces have dimensions

`a = dim Q_A >= 1`, `b = dim Q_B >= 1`.

Assume only that the composite admits the local product sector `Q_A tensor Q_B` and that all declared product effects act on that sector in the usual bilinear way. Then local quotient data alone do **not** determine a unique composite space.

For every integer `h >= 0`, define

`Q_AB^(h) = (Q_A tensor Q_B) direct_sum H_h`,

where `H_h` is any `h`-dimensional vector space, and extend every declared product effect to vanish on `H_h`.

Then:

1. every `Q_AB^(h)` gives exactly the same statistics for every declared local/product effect;
2. the composite dimensions are `dim Q_AB^(h) = ab + h`;
3. different `h` therefore give inequivalent composite distinction capacities while preserving all local quotient data and all declared product statistics.

Hence no rule using only the present local quotient data can derive a unique composite dimension or unique tensor/composition law.

## Proof
The product sector has dimension `ab`. For any product effect `e_A tensor e_B`, define its extension to `Q_AB^(h)` by

`(e_A tensor e_B)(x,z) = (e_A tensor e_B)(x)`

for `x in Q_A tensor Q_B` and `z in H_h`.

Thus every declared product effect annihilates `H_h`. Consequently all product-effect evaluations on `(x,z)` depend only on `x` and are independent of `z`. Therefore all local/product operational data are identical for every choice of `h`, while the total composite dimensions differ by `h`. This proves non-uniqueness.

## Smallest decisive witness
Take `a=b=1`.

- Composite C0: `Q_AB^(0) = R`, dimension 1.
- Composite C1: `Q_AB^(1) = R direct_sum R`, dimension 2.
- The sole product effect reads the first coordinate and annihilates the second.

The two composites have identical local/product statistics but different global distinction dimension. Thus the obstruction exists already at the smallest nonzero local dimensions.

## Adversarial/edge cases
- `h=0`: recovers the product-generated/local-tomographic candidate.
- `h>0`: hidden global sector invisible to declared product effects.
- Redundant local effects: do not remove the construction; they only reduce local quotient dimensions before composition.
- Full local effect spaces: still do not see `H_h` if only product-generated composite effects are declared.
- Norm changes: irrelevant to this linear indistinguishability theorem.
- Reversible local coordinate changes: act inside the product sector and leave the existence of an invisible direct-sum sector unchanged.
- Pure/mixed labels: irrelevant unless PDT supplies additional state-cone structure that constrains `H_h`.
- Markovian/non-Markovian dynamics and environment records: can make hidden sectors operationally accessible only after extra transformation/effect structure is specified; that is additional composite theory data, not a consequence of the local quotients.

## What is falsified
**FALSIFIED:** “PDT's present local resource-relative quotient spaces uniquely force the physical composite.”

Also falsified is any dimension formula such as `D_AB=D_A D_B` claimed as unconditional from local quotient data alone. The multiplicative law remains valid only under the explicit product-generated/local-tomographic hypothesis proved earlier.

## Surviving theorem
If PDT explicitly postulates or independently derives that the complete composite effect space is generated by product effects (equivalently, no nonzero globally hidden sector survives product tomography in this finite-dimensional setting), then

`Q_AB ~= Q_A tensor Q_B`, and `D_AB = D_A D_B`.

That is a **CONDITIONAL** composition theorem, not yet a PDT-native derivation of the physical hypothesis.

## Prior-art boundary
The obstruction is established operational/GPT territory. Failure of local tomography is known to permit global degrees of freedom invisible to local measurements; real-vector-space quantum theory is a standard example and bilocal tomography has been studied explicitly (Hardy & Wootters, 2010). Modern GPT work likewise treats tomographic locality as an additional physical principle rather than a consequence of arbitrary local state/effect spaces. Therefore the abstract no-go must not be claimed as PDT novelty.

Relevant sources checked this cycle:
- L. Hardy and W. K. Wootters, *Limited Holism and Real-Vector-Space Quantum Theory*, arXiv:1005.4870 (2010).
- C. M. Lee and J. Barrett, *Computation in generalised probabilistic theories*, arXiv:1412.8671 (tomographic locality as an explicit physical assumption).
- T. S. Lismer et al., *Experimental Test of the Principle of Tomographic Locality*, arXiv:2506.07775 (2025), including the operational signature of global distinctions invisible to separable/local measurements.

## Consequence for PDT-II
Target (1) is now sharply constrained. A genuinely PDT-native composition law must derive an additional physical principle that excludes or quantitatively fixes hidden global sectors. Merely tensoring the already-defined local quotients is a choice of composition, not a derivation.

The next prove-or-falsify target should therefore test candidate PDT-native exclusion principles (for example a resource-completeness or operational-generation principle) and reject any principle that simply restates local tomography in PDT vocabulary.
