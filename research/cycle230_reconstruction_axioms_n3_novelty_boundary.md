## Prior-art boundary
Established quantum-reconstruction programmes already show that packages of operational axioms can strongly constrain or reconstruct finite-dimensional quantum theory. In particular, reconstruction work using tomographic locality, a subspace axiom and continuous reversibility identifies quantum theory; spectrality plus strong symmetry characterizes simple Euclidean Jordan-algebra state spaces (plus simplices); purification has established consequences for composite/process structure. Therefore merely renaming any such axiom as a 'distinction principle' would not constitute a PDT-native derivation.

## Exact logical test for n=3
Let A(n) denote any candidate axiom package whose clauses are dimension-uniform: each clause is stated for every finite capacity n and contains no dimension-specific physical invariant. If models satisfying A(n) exist for multiple n (as they do in ordinary finite-dimensional quantum theory and in broad GPT/Jordan families), then A alone cannot prove `n=3` as the unique physical capacity. Formally, if A(n1) and A(n2) are satisfiable for distinct n1 != n2, the conclusion `A => n=3` is invalid. This is a model-theoretic counterexample, not a numerical approximation.

The standard complex quantum family supplies witnesses for n=1,...,12 (indeed arbitrary finite n) for the usual dimension-uniform quantum reconstruction properties once the corresponding system exists. Hence importing those properties cannot by itself select n=3. Any apparent n=3 conclusion must enter through an additional hypothesis, boundary condition, physical invariant, or identification of n with some other quantity.

## Stress-test ledger n=1..12
For each n=1,...,12 ask whether the candidate clause itself syntactically/operationally singles out n. Result: NO for the dimension-uniform reconstruction clauses above. n=1 is degenerate; n=2,...,12 remain admissible system capacities in their established theories. This is an exact logical stress test; no simulated data are claimed.

## Surviving theorem obligation
A non-circular PDT-native n=3 result must have the form

`PDT primitives + independently justified physical boundary/resource facts => invariant F(n) uniquely extremized/closed/consistent at n=3`,

where neither the primitives nor the boundary facts encode n=3, complex Hilbert dimension 3, Bloch/Jordan structure, SU(3), qutrit composition, or an equivalent desired result.

Required falsification protocol for any future F(n):
1. state hypotheses without n-specific language;
2. compute/prove F(n) for n=1..12;
3. search degeneracies and alternate norms/groups/compositions;
4. test whether another n has the same property;
5. identify whether the key axiom is already a reconstruction axiom in GPT/Jordan/quantum foundations;
6. only then consider novelty.

## Same-input prediction consequence
A PDT-vs-QM difference obtained by imposing an extra reconstruction axiom on PDT but not on the comparator is not a same-input prediction. Identical microscopic preparation, dynamics/couplings, measurement, ancillary permissions and resource window must be held fixed.

## Next strongest attack
Derive, rather than assume, a PDT-native quantitative invariant from the existing distinction/refinement/revelation primitives. The first useful candidate must be dimension-uniform in definition and then be adversarially evaluated at n=1..12 before any n=3 claim.
