# PDT-II Cycle 222 — Control-Architecture No-Go

Date: 2026-09-18

## Target attacked

PDT-II target (1): a PDT-native composition law, following Cycles 220–221 where scalar and finite-vector resource summaries failed to determine joint admissibility.

## Candidate principle under test

> **Candidate C222.** The structured resource state `r`, controller state/dynamics `K`, and local admissible algebras `A_X,A_Y` determine the composite admissible algebra `A_XY` without separately specifying the allowed system-controller interaction architecture.

Status: **FALSIFIED**.

## Exact hypotheses

Let X and Y be finite classical systems. Let the controller/resource R have a fixed state r and fixed autonomous dynamics K. The local admissible algebras A_X and A_Y are fixed. A control architecture C is a specified set of allowed joint maps on X×Y×R. The induced joint admissible algebra A_XY(r,K,C) consists of outcome functions implementable by finite compositions of maps in C followed by allowed readout.

Candidate C222 asserts that A_XY is a function only of (r,K,A_X,A_Y).

## Smallest decisive witness

Take X=Y={0,1}. Let R be a one-state controller, so the resource state and autonomous controller dynamics are identical in both models. Give both systems the same local readout algebra generated by x and y.

Architecture C_local permits only independent local maps/readouts. Architecture C_parity additionally permits the joint reversible gate

    G(x,y) = (x, x XOR y)

followed by the same local readout of the second bit.

Under C_parity, parity x XOR y is jointly accessible. Under C_local, no protocol generated solely by independent local maps can implement this joint interaction/readout as a single controller-mediated transformation. Thus the induced reachable joint-operation sets differ although (r,K,A_X,A_Y) are identical.

The witness is deliberately classical: the obstruction precedes quantum structure and therefore cannot be repaired by choosing a norm, Hilbert field, pure/mixed-state convention, or quantum tensor product.

## Theorem 222.1 — interaction-architecture necessity

For a general operational theory, structured resource state plus autonomous controller dynamics plus local admissibility data do not uniquely determine composite admissibility. In particular, if two permitted interaction sets C1,C2 act on the same X,Y,R and generate different reachable joint maps, then

    A_XY(r,K,C1) != A_XY(r,K,C2)

for some allowed readout whenever the differing reachable maps are operationally separable by that readout.

### Proof

Choose T in Reach(C2)\Reach(C1) that is separated by an allowed effect e from every C1-reachable map on at least one input z. Then e∘T is an admissible joint outcome function under C2 and is not implementable under C1. All non-architectural data are held fixed by construction. Therefore no universal map (r,K,A_X,A_Y)->A_XY can exist without hypotheses that determine the reachable interaction set. QED.

Status: **PROVED** (elementary operational theorem).

## Dimension stress

The binary witness embeds into every finite local cardinality n>=2 by restricting to labels {0,1} and leaving labels 2,...,n-1 fixed. Hence the obstruction persists exactly for n=2,...,12 and all n>=2. n=1 is correctly degenerate: no nontrivial binary joint gate exists.

The same embedding persists in larger vector, probabilistic, and quantum models whenever the theory contains the corresponding classical face/subtheory.

## What survives

A defensible PDT composition object must include at least

    (r, K, C, A_X, A_Y) -> A_XY(r,K,C),

where C specifies or is itself derived as the physically allowed interaction/control architecture.

But defining A_XY as the closure of operations reachable from C is **not yet a PDT-native predictive law**: it is an operational construction. PDT must derive a nontrivial restriction or selection rule for C from independent physical principles before composition becomes predictive.

## Consequences for remaining targets

* **n=3:** cannot be derived from resource/controller structure alone if the interaction architecture remains free; different C can produce different joint distinction structures with the same non-architectural inputs.
* **same-input PDT vs QM prediction:** a difference obtained merely by choosing a different C is not a same-input prediction unless PDT independently predicts that C while QM is evaluated with the identical microscopic interaction specification.
* **gravity/capacity:** a gravity/capacity scalar cannot repair this obstruction unless it derives the allowed coupling architecture rather than merely assigning a budget.

## Prior-art boundary

This theorem is not promoted as novel. Operational/process theories, quantum resource theories of channels, controllability theory, and constrained dynamics all explicitly treat allowed transformations/interactions as structural data. Complete families of resource monotones characterize a *specified* transformation preorder; they do not create that preorder. Recent resource-theory literature likewise distinguishes state resources from dynamical resources and free superchannels.

Relevant prior art checked in this cycle:

1. Gour & Scandolo, *Dynamical Resources*, Int. J. Quantum Inf. 19 (2021), arXiv:2101.01552 — resource theories of channels and free superchannels; complete monotone families are relative to specified free transformations.
2. Datta et al., *Is There a Finite Complete Set of Monotones in Any Quantum Resource Theory?*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 130, 240204 (2023) — finite monotone incompleteness under broad hypotheses.
3. Zanoni & Scandolo, *Choi-defined resource theories*, Phys. Rev. A 111, 062407 (2025) — operation structure and complete families in specified Choi-defined resource theories.

## Research rule carried forward

Do not call a chosen coupling set a PDT prediction. The next admissibility candidate must derive a restriction on C from independently stated PDT hypotheses and must be attacked against classical faces, alternative reversible groups, restricted controls, environment-assisted operations, memory/non-Markovian controls, and catalytic/thermodynamic settings.
