# Cycle 201 — Anti-exchange does not select a PDT-native activation/composition law

## Target
Strongest unresolved PDT-II target: derive a PDT-native composition/resource-activation law after Cycle 200 showed that generic closure axioms do not select one.

## Candidate principle attacked
A natural strengthening is to require a resource-activation closure `cl` on a finite distinction set X to satisfy:

1. `cl(∅)=∅` (zero closure),
2. extensivity: `A ⊆ cl(A)`,
3. monotonicity: `A ⊆ B => cl(A) ⊆ cl(B)`,
4. idempotence: `cl(cl(A))=cl(A)`,
5. anti-exchange: for closed A and distinct x,y outside A, `x ∈ cl(A∪{y}) => y ∉ cl(A∪{x})`.

Motivation tested: anti-exchange might encode directional/irreversible physical revelation strongly enough to select a unique nonlinear PDT activation law.

## Exact counterexample
Take X={a,b,c}.

### Closure C0 (identity)
`C0(S)=S` for every S⊆X.

### Closure C1 (one-way activation)
Let the only nontrivial implication be `{a,b} -> c`:

`C1(S)=S∪{c}` if `{a,b}⊆S`, and `C1(S)=S` otherwise.

Both C0 and C1 satisfy zero closure, extensivity, monotonicity and idempotence.

C1 also satisfies anti-exchange. The only generated element is c, and c never generates a or b. Hence if x is newly generated by adding y, the reverse generation cannot occur. Direct exhaustive enumeration over all closed A and all distinct x,y outside A verifies the axiom.

Yet the two laws disagree on the same joint resource input:

`C0({a,b})={a,b}`,

`C1({a,b})={a,b,c}`.

Thus anti-exchange still permits both zero joint generation and genuine joint generation.

## Minimality
With |X|≤2 there is no room for two independently supplied triggers plus a third newly generated distinction. Therefore |X|=3 is the smallest witness for this specific two-resource synergy-selection question.

## Dimension stress test
The witness embeds unchanged into every finite ambient distinction set of size n≥3 by adjoining inert elements fixed by both closures. Therefore it covers n=3,...,12 exactly and all higher finite n. n=1,2 are degenerate for the stated two-trigger/one-new-output witness.

## Surviving theorem
**Anti-exchange non-selection theorem.** Closure axioms plus anti-exchange do not determine whether a pair of resources jointly generates a new distinction. Consequently they cannot derive a unique PDT composition/activation law.

The theorem is structural and independent of norm choice, state purity, reversible-group representation, or Markovian versus non-Markovian dynamics because none of those structures appears in the hypotheses. Adding such structure can constrain a closure only through an additional compatibility axiom that must itself be physically derived and tested.

## Prior-art boundary
Anti-exchange closure operators are the standard mathematical definition of convex geometries/antimatroid duals. Therefore adopting anti-exchange itself is IMPORTED/KNOWN mathematics, not PDT novelty. The present result is used only as a PDT-specific no-go audit: this known axiom cannot perform the missing physical selection.

Operational/GPT and compositional resource-theory literature already treats composition and allowed transformations as additional physical structure. PDT therefore cannot claim novelty merely by choosing a known closure/convex-geometry axiom.

## Consequences for remaining PDT-II targets
- PDT-native composition law: OPEN. Anti-exchange does not select it.
- Non-circular n=3 derivation: OPEN. The smallest counterexample occurs exactly at three distinctions, so anti-exchange cannot supply the missing n=3 selection.
- Same-input PDT/QM prediction: OPEN. No probability functional follows from this closure principle.
- Resource revelation law: CONDITIONAL. Once a concrete activation law is supplied, its closure consequences can be proved, but the law is not selected by these axioms.
- Distinctive inequality: OPEN. Any inequality relying on anti-exchange alone is not yet PDT-specific.
- Gravity/capacity: OPEN; no derivation attempted because the required microscopic cost/capacity law is absent.
